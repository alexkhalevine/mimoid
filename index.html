              Read each prompt aloud and record it — the more you record, the closer the clone sounds.
              You don't need to do anything else afterward: once a sample is saved it's used
              automatically. In Talk mode, click the 🔊 on any reply to hear it in your
              voice, and replies to questions you ask by microphone play back on their own. Heads up: the
              very first playback downloads the voice model (~1.8 GB), so it's slow once, then fast.
              For the best clone, record somewhere quiet (no fan/AC hum, no echo-y room), keep the mic at
              a consistent distance, and speak at your normal, natural pace rather than a slow reading
              voice.
