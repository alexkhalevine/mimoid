# Font licenses

All fonts bundled here are self-hosted for offline use (no CDN at runtime)
and licensed under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org),
which permits this. Each family's exact license text, with its own copyright
line, is alongside it:

| Files | Family | License |
|---|---|---|
| `space-grotesk-variable.woff2` | [Space Grotesk](https://github.com/floriankarsten/space-grotesk) | `OFL-space-grotesk.txt` |
| `ibm-plex-mono-400.woff2`, `ibm-plex-mono-500.woff2` | [IBM Plex Mono](https://github.com/IBM/plex) | `OFL-ibm-plex-mono.txt` |
| `spectral-400.woff2`, `spectral-500.woff2`, `spectral-600.woff2`, `spectral-italic-400.woff2`, `spectral-italic-500.woff2` | [Spectral](https://github.com/productiontype/Spectral) | `OFL-spectral.txt` |

None of these files were modified from upstream beyond subsetting/converting
to WOFF2. OFL's one real restriction that matters here: the font files
themselves can't be sold on their own, separate from the app -- bundled with
the app, as they are, is exactly what the license allows.
